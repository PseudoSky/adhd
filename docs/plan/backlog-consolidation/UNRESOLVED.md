## Scope finding (corrects a spec premise)

**S11 R1's stated premise is inaccurate.** R1 says the claim interval is "RED today: auditTrail shows no claim/renew/release rows." A live probe of the shipped build shows `claim` **does** append an audit row (`{"action":"claimed","to":"<claimedBy>",...}`, visible via `get fields:["auditTrail"]`). The genuine remaining gap is narrower and is what the demo asserts: **no durable claim *interval* is persisted** (the row lacks `paths[]`, `sessionId`, and `leaseExpiry`) **and** the `reservations` view / `timeline` verb do not exist. Shipped evidence: `write/claim.ts` calls `writeAudit` with `action: 'claimed' | 'reclaimed-stale' | 'renewed' | 'released'`; the only stored claim state is `issue.meta.metadata.claimedBy`/`claimedAt` (`write/claim-lease.ts` `extractClaimMeta`). **Applied:** the premise is corrected in `docs/plan/backlog-consolidation/SPEC-SET.md` S11.R1 and `tmp/backlog-consolidation/partial-disposition.md` §9.1 (and its §4 `46cf1086` row). `DEMO.md` §3 already states the gap this way.
